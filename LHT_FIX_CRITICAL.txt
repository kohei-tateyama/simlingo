CRITICAL FIX: Left-Hand Traffic Not Working
==============================================================================

PROBLEM:
--------
Even with metadata in XML and route parser updates, LHT still didn't work.
The leaderboard logs showed "> Configuring LEFT-HAND traffic" but vehicles
were still driving on the right side.

ROOT CAUSE:
-----------
**WRONG:** `self.traffic_manager.global_lane_offset = -3.5` (attribute assignment)
**CORRECT:** `self.traffic_manager.global_lane_offset(-3.5)` (method call)

The Traffic Manager's `global_lane_offset` is a METHOD, not an attribute!

FIXED IN:
---------
✓ leaderboard/leaderboard/leaderboard_evaluator.py (line ~276)
✓ leaderboard/leaderboard/leaderboard_evaluator_local.py (line ~291)

Changed from:
  self.traffic_manager.global_lane_offset = lane_offset

To:
  self.traffic_manager.global_lane_offset(float(lane_offset))

   bash script/test_data_agent_multicamera_timeout_all_carla0916.sh

   "> Configuring LEFT-HAND traffic (lane_offset=-3.5)"
